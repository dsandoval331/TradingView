RESEARCH RULES
1. Do not optimize thresholds simply to maximize historical FF%.
2. Primary benchmark remains +0.50% favorable before -0.50% adverse.
3. Report N with every rate.
4. Split BULL/BEAR after establishing overall relationship.
5. Check checkpoint stability where applicable.
6. Check temporal robustness before production promotion.
7. Pre-signal variables use only information available by signal time.
8. Test individual factors before high-dimensional interactions.
9. Do not promote small-N high-performing buckets.
10. Keep Signal Quality (entry-known) separate from Trade Health (post-entry).
11. Preserve null/rejected findings to prevent repeated data mining.
12. Freeze model versions before final validation.
13. Validate frozen candidates with out-of-sample/forward data rather than retuning.
